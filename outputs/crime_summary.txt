The dataset contains information about crimes in Chicago for the year 2026. The main columns in the dataset are:

- **ID**: Unique identifier for each crime record.
- **Case Number**: Case reference number.
- **Date**: Date and time of the crime.
- **Block**: Approximate address of the crime.
- **IUCR**: Illinois Uniform Crime Reporting code.
- **Primary Type**: Primary classification of the crime.
- **Description**: Detailed description of the crime.
- **Location Description**: Type of location where the crime occurred.
- **Arrest**: Indicates if an arrest was made (true/false).
- **Domestic**: Indicates if the crime was domestic-related (true/false).
- **Beat, District, Ward, Community Area**: Administrative and geographic divisions.
- **FBI Code**: FBI classification code.
- **X Coordinate, Y Coordinate**: Spatial coordinates.
- **Year**: Year of the crime.
- **Updated On**: Last update timestamp.
- **Latitude, Longitude**: Geographic coordinates.
- **Location**: Combined latitude and longitude in POINT format.
